## What is complete and actually checked

### Theory and finite counterexamples

`docs/theory_v1.md` and `paper/sections/theory_v1.tex` contain internal proof drafts for:

- exact sound-set existence, open/closed completeness endpoints, and the equality between the
  infimum completeness threshold and exact fiber defect;
- deterministic data processing and the observation floor;
- deterministic common-action impossibility;
- properly qualified randomized fixed-fiber statements;
- closed-convex least-intervention projection; and
- the finite-action pointwise expected-loss sandwich, strong-slack equality, and LP dual.

`docs/theory_extensions_2026-08-22.md` and the included proof appendix now add two sharper
boundaries. For countable physical-state families and arbitrary measurable stochastic code spaces,
a common almost-sure separator exists exactly when every required-safe law is mutually singular to
every unsafe law; the exact pairwise total separator error is `1-TV`, Markov postprocessing cannot
improve separability, and disjoint safe-action sets impose the same TV lower bound on summed
randomized-policy violations. A common-prior Pinsker corollary and the nondegenerate-Gaussian
impossibility consequence are stated with their pointwise qualifications. For compact convex
actuator sets, Helly's theorem compresses common-action feasibility and exact geometric slack to
at most `p+1` states, sharpened to affine dimension `q+1`, with a sharp full-dimensional witness.
Independent internal theorem audits found no counterexample; external proof and novelty review
remain required before promotion.

The same package now includes a smooth compact-support construction showing that expected
reconstruction and latent-prediction loss can vanish while a worst-case fiber defect stays fixed.
Its scaling is analytic and regression-tested; it is a separation result, not evidence that
trained models exhibit the failure at meaningful prevalence.

`docs/theory_v2_dynamic.md` and `paper/sections/theory_v2_dynamic.tex` contain a candidate finite
history-tree extension. Its executable audit deliberately separates three objects that cannot be
interchanged:

- robust optimal-margin regret `rho`, which measures loss relative to each history's best Bellman
  margin;
- first-action obstruction, which tests whether individually viable histories in one code fiber
  admit a common viable action; and
- retained viable-history coverage under a chosen representation-level policy.

The dynamic candidate now also contains the exact reachable-history-set Bellman recursion
`W_s^R(S)`, its stagewise representation-refinement inequality, recursive shortfall, and the
downward-closed family of jointly retainable viable histories. Its memoized finite oracle is checked
against independent whole-policy enumeration. Exact endpoint decisions accept only finite inputs
unchanged by binary64 conversion and fail closed on underflow or round-tie inputs.

The finite Bellman loss and composition inequalities pass their current checks, but the red team in
`docs/theory_v2_redteam_2026-08-22.md` found direct novelty overlap with approximate information
states and showed that minimizing `rho` alone is not an exact safety-sufficiency guarantee. The
candidate TeX is therefore excluded from `paper/main.tex`.

The complete-finite-domain route now has a fail-closed verifier that validates the supplied finite
history metric; derives its cover radius, Euclidean representation and sampled-$Q$ Lipschitz
constants, and uniform sampled-$Q$ error from the enumeration; then outward-encloses every
$Q$-coordinate difference within the operational latent radius, including stored-float
subtraction roundoff.
This closes the arithmetic route only for a complete finite domain; it does not certify unseen or
continuous populations.

The red-team records document corrected quantifiers, randomization semantics, measurability,
normalization, floating-point endpoints, and continuous-action caveats. The executed theory
notebook exhaustively checks 648 deterministic representation/threshold cases, 648 deterministic
coarsenings, 486 closed plus 486 open stochastic certificate cases, 81 one-hot reductions, 36 exact
TV/error pairs, 81 randomized-action tradeoffs, 243 Markov postprocessings, 500 interval-Helly
families, endpoint examples, a common-prior KL fixture, and 100 randomized finite-action loss
matrices. Stochastic kernels and Markov products are validated in exact rational arithmetic before
guarded binary64 reporting, and consecutive fresh executions are byte-identical. Separate executed
notebooks check the smooth construction and finite dynamic oracle.

These statements remain marked `theorem_needs_proof` in `paper/claims.toml` because executable and
independent internal audits do not replace external proof and novelty review. More importantly, the exact-fiber statements are
foundational and overlap established abstraction, refinement, action-sufficiency, and belief-shield
ideas. They should not headline an ICML submission without a nontrivial approximate or dynamic
extension.

### E0 measurement semantics

The dependency-free metric and benchmark code now covers exact finite fibers, radius-indexed

finite-radius common-action conflicts, and controlled double-integrator examples.

The executed E0 notebook checks all of its machine-readable gates. Salient planted controls are:

- exact defect zero for an injective finite representation;
- a known robust near-collision threshold and joint code/radius rescaling invariance;

- saturated-sensor static defect `0.5`;

This is a semantic validation of the estimators, not a proof of population coverage. The full E0

### Finite dynamic bridge

The registered three-step oracle in `src/latent_safety/benchmarks/dynamic_oracle.py` exhaustively
enumerates all branches from two hand-selected same-frame/different-velocity roots in each
controlled domain. In both cart and pendulum, the exact current-frame code has positive
first-action obstruction and no globally viability-preserving code policy on the enumerated game;
privileged kinematic state and a causal two-frame pixel history have zero obstruction and retain
both viable roots. The audit also reports robust optimal-margin regret, Q-approximation error,
realized code-policy loss, and local/global Bellman bounds separately.

This is a registered finite floating-point theorem fixture—not learned-model evidence, a statement
about the training distribution, or a population/deployment certificate. The cart roots are outside
the noise-free behavior generator's velocity envelope. See `docs/dynamic_oracle_bridge.md` for the
exact scope and numbers.

## GPU pilot handoff

The recommended decision gate is:

`2 domains × 2 model families × 2 history modes × 1 latent dimension × 4 safety arms × 3 paired seeds = 96`

Each domain contributes 48 tasks. The modes are `stack_h1` and `stack_h4`: the first is a true
single-frame/memoryless control and the second receives four frames. The plan records the mode,
underlying encoder, and history length separately and includes the mode in the output path.

The `stack_h1`/`stack_h4` contrast is capacity-matched through a fixed-width
`(last, last-first)` summary. The h1 difference block is zero, while h4 receives motion
information. The notebook asserts equal parameter counts and run metadata records them.

The optional expansion adds `gru_h4` and latent dimension 4, yielding 144 tasks per domain and 288
total. It should run only if the 96-task gate survives its stopping rules. Both designs remain a
narrow synthetic benchmark and are unrelated to the proposal's historical 96-model study.

Generate and inspect the immutable task plan before allocating GPUs:

```bash
mkdir -p runs/plans runs/slurm
python scripts/plan_e1_sweep.py \
  --grid configs/e1_world_models/decisive_cart_grid.toml \
  --output runs/plans/e1_decisive_cart.json
python scripts/plan_e1_sweep.py \
  --grid configs/e1_world_models/decisive_pendulum_grid.toml \
  --output runs/plans/e1_decisive_pendulum.json
python scripts/run_e1_sweep_task.py \
  --plan runs/plans/e1_decisive_cart.json \
  --index 0 \
  --device cuda \
  --dry-run
```

Run one task locally on a GPU:

```bash
python scripts/run_e1_sweep_task.py \
  --plan runs/plans/e1_decisive_cart.json \
  --index 0 \
  --device cuda
```

Or submit the checked-in Slurm array after adapting partition/account/module directives for the
cluster:

```bash
sbatch experiments/e1_world_models/slurm_decisive_cart_array.sh
sbatch experiments/e1_world_models/slurm_decisive_pendulum_array.sh
```

Each successful indexed task trains one configuration and then writes
`latent_safety_audit.json`. Preserve failed outputs and scheduler logs; do not rerun into the same
directory. The checked-in array permits at most eight concurrent tasks and requests one GPU, four
CPUs, 32 GB RAM, and four hours per task. These are starting estimates, not measured resource
requirements for every cluster; submitting both domain arrays permits up to sixteen tasks at once.
A write-producing indexed run requires the plan and source-config hashes, canonical regenerated
factorial expansion, and exact clean Git revision to match. Generate plans only after committing
and regenerate them after every code change. Audit failures/skips remain fail-closed in the run
manifest, while a separate sweep-task manifest records the completed audit views. The
`--allow-unversioned` escape hatch is for disposable engineering checks only.

### Frozen exploratory analysis choices

The decisive cart and pendulum grids inherit the same frozen analysis choices:

- relative audit radii `[0, 0.01, 0.02, 0.05, 0.10]` times the calibration-only median pairwise
  latent distance;
- primary relative radius `0.05`;
- validation safety-budget threshold `0.10` for static defect;
- 10,000 paired bootstrap resamples with RNG seed `20260821`; and
- validation as the selection split.

The pilot advances only if E0 controls remain green and at least one safety arm improves the
matched-utility validation frontier. Three seeds are exploratory. A confirmatory comparison needs
the selected eight-seed design, or a preregistered precision justification, plus all failures and
timeouts.

One important procedural distinction remains. The legacy E1 exploratory runner computes a test audit
after training; excluding those values from its analysis cannot prevent a researcher from seeing
them, so that pilot remains exploratory. The E2 preconfirmation runner instead materializes only
train and validation, performs physical-profile evaluation only after checkpoint selection, and
records that calibration and final test were never opened. Frozen weights and analysis code must
still precede the separate, one-time confirmatory test unblinding.

   on first-action obstruction and retained viability, state the relationship to robust
   optimal-margin regret precisely, and compare theorem-by-theorem with approximate information
   states and feedback refinement.

   compactness, and policy-randomization semantics.
4. Re-run the novelty comparison against feedback refinement, state-action abstractions, POMDP

   headline theorem.

### Empirics

1. Execute the 96-task two-domain decision pilot, retain every run state, and aggregate only
   complete paired-seed blocks on validation. Do not launch the optional 288-task expansion unless
   the preregistered gate passes.
2. Validate the implemented ground-truth-state, visible-observation, and history-versus-memoryless
   controls at scale; the frozen controlled-Dubins third domain now passes its CPU integration smoke
   but still needs the preconfirmation information and coverage gates.
3. Reproduce the strongest directly relevant baselines before presenting a new loss as state of the
   art; include the scalar-`h` append/prediction oracle as a mandatory control.
4. Use preconfirmation data only to freeze the positive learned-arm weights for the exact 192-task
   matrix, then run seeds 100–107 and unblind final test results once.
5. Enter downstream E5 only after a stable E2 frontier exists; test certificate coverage, common
   action feasibility, closed-loop violations, return, and control effort with the same frozen
   encoders.

### Paper discipline

Promote a result only by updating `paper/claims.toml` with its producing command, immutable
manifest, evidence path, and reviewer. The current credible paper path is either a novel
approximate/dynamic theorem, a finite-data certificate with a real search guarantee, or a strong
observation-controlled matched-utility result that predicts downstream safety. Static exact-fiber
lemmas and CPU smoke metrics are not enough by themselves.
