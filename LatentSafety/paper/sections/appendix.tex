\section{Claim and artifact contract}

Every theorem and empirical statement is registered in \texttt{claims.toml}.  A claim moves to
verified status only with a producing command, immutable manifest, evidence artifact, and an
independent collaborator rerun.  Expected losses, finite-reference witnesses, exact verification,
and probabilistic tolerance statements use separate labels throughout code and text.

\section{Remaining proof and extension obligations}

The exact static, deterministic data-processing, action-conflict, common-action projection,
finite-action, and smooth average-loss-separation results in the main text still require
independent line-by-line review.  Extensions
not yet proved include stochastic encoders and overlap defects; approximate latent neighborhoods;
measurable selectors across codes; cover bounds with metric-entropy assumptions; and end-to-end
composition of representation,
dynamics, specification, solver, and feedback errors.  These open items are not implied by the
set-theoretic theorems.

The separately compiled dynamic candidate is intentionally not included in this manuscript.  Its
finite Bellman bounds are internally checked, but robust optimal-margin regret, first-action safety
obstruction, and retained viable coverage have different semantics, and the package still requires
independent proof and novelty review against approximate information states, symbolic safety
synthesis, and belief-support shielding.
