\section{Limitations and broader impact}

Fiber verification can be as hard as neural verification; an order-statistic wrapper still needs
the violation oracle.  Static separation gives neither Markov dynamics nor long-horizon invariance.
Learned specifications require error composition; transfer and operational radii remain deployment
choices.  The stochastic separator theorem uses countably many physical states and an almost-sure
criterion; pairwise singularity need not combine into one separator for an uncountable family.
Its Gaussian consequence concerns sampled full-support posterior laws, whereas our experiments use
posterior means.  The continuous-action compression requires exact nonempty closed-convex safe sets
inside a compact convex actuator set; its action-space distance is not a calibrated violation cost,
and numerical or sampled feasibility checks are not population certificates.

Safe-action sufficiency is relative to the registered safety margin, horizon, continuation rule,
and finite action or skill library.  A sampled library cannot support a claim for a continuous
action space without verified inner optimization.  Simulator branching supplies privileged
counterfactual labels unavailable in many real systems; predicted teachers add model error, and a
percentile validation gate is not a uniform certificate.  Empirical neighborhoods approximate
fibers only under a declared geometry and coverage regime and can be gamed by representation
rescaling, dispersion, or pooling.  Matched-neighborhood-mass, physical groups, retention metrics,
and fresh-head tests diagnose but do not eliminate these limitations.

Cosmos, V-JEPA, and DINO differ in architecture, parameter count, data, input interface, and native
objective.  Their cross-checkpoint comparison is descriptive, while repair conclusions are paired
within family; causal claims about pretraining objectives require the proposed matched-backbone and
matched-data ablation.  Native reconstruction, prediction, and dense-feature metrics are also not
directly commensurate.  The primary domains are simulated manipulation and navigation, so even a
positive result would not establish real-robot, driving, or specification-universal safety.

We report no completed pretrained-encoder audit, internal repair, confirmatory comparison,
foundation-scale downstream result, or population certificate.  The existing CPU profile, FCSRL,
world-model, and Dubins paths establish precursor plumbing only.  Dynamic controls use hand-selected
roots and known-dynamics profiles.  Audits are not guarantees; releases will record witnesses,
failed controls, coverage, and uncertainty assumptions.
