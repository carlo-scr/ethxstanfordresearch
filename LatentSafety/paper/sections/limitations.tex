\section{Limitations and broader impact}

Worst-case fiber verification can be as hard as neural verification; a dimension-free
order-statistic wrapper does not remove the need to compute its violation oracle.  Static
separation does not establish Markov dynamics, controller feasibility, or long-horizon invariance.
Results relative to a learned safety specification must compose its estimation error, and results
relative to one known specification need not transfer to another.  Operational radii and state
metrics encode deployment choices that must be reported.

Better representation audits could reduce unjustified safety claims and reveal silent
conservatism.  Conversely, a finite-data audit can itself be mistaken for a formal guarantee.  We
will release witness semantics, failed controls, and uncertainty assumptions prominently and avoid
describing probabilistic or empirical results as absolute safety certification.
