\section{Auditing and learning}

\paragraph{Finite-sample witnesses.}
Let $z_i=R(x_i)$, $h_i=h(x_i)$, and let $q_i(a)$ be the declared finite-action safety margin, with
$q_i(a)\geq0$ denoting feasibility of action $a$ at sample $i$.  At a frozen radius $\delta$, the
static finite-reference witness is
\[
 \widehat\varepsilon_\delta
 =\max\!\left(\{h_i:h_i\geq0,\ \exists j\text{ with }h_j<0,
 \ \|z_i-z_j\|_2\leq\delta\}\cup\{0\}\right).
\]
Every returned pair is traceable to sample identifiers.  Conditional on exact margins and
distances, an observed pair lower-bounds the corresponding population radius supremum; absence of
a pair supplies no population upper bound.  An unthresholded nearest unsafe neighbor does not
witness an exact collision and is retained only as a sampling-dependent diagnostic.

For action sufficiency, first restrict the reference population to individually viable indices
$\mathcal I_0=\{j:\max_a q_j(a)\geq0\}$.  For each $i\in\mathcal I_0$, center the viable
neighborhood
$N_i^{\mathcal V}(\delta)=\{j\in\mathcal I_0:\|z_j-z_i\|_2\leq\delta\}$ and retain it only when
$|N_i^{\mathcal V}(\delta)|\geq2$.  Its required common-action violation is
\[
 v_i(\delta)=\left[-\max_a\min_{j\in N_i^{\mathcal V}(\delta)}q_j(a)\right]_+.
\]
This detects conflicts involving more than two states and records maximizing witnesses, eligible
denominator, and conflict fraction; a trajectory with no eligible center fails rather than scoring
zero.  The primary summary is the arithmetic mean across trajectories of their linearly
interpolated $0.95$ quantiles; seeds, not trajectories, are paired for inference.  A repair weight
is eligible only if every validation stratum satisfies the preregistered within-family native-
utility margin, retains the required center and trajectory coverage, and preserves the improvement
sign under a radius chosen to match the frozen encoder's nonself neighborhood mass.  Exact gates,
radii, and multiplicity corrections freeze in the foundation-study protocol before test access.
Trajectories are resampled only within seed for tail estimation.  The global maximum remains a lower
witness, not the sole endpoint.  Continuous-action claims require verified, not grid-only, optimization.

\paragraph{Operational calibration.}
Raw latent radii change under code rescaling.  A calibration split may define an exploratory scale
convention; robust or certified semantics require a deployment-grounded uncertainty set or verified
set propagation.  We retain
translation, rotation, rescaling, and irrelevant-coordinate controls.  A radius expressed as a
fraction of calibration-set median pairwise distance is only a scale-normalized exploratory view;
the conclusion must also survive a frozen radius curve, matched-neighborhood-mass analysis, and
physically generated perturbation or alias pairs.  Exact-code synthetic tests serve as unit tests,
not evidence that continuous neural encoders collide in practice.

\paragraph{Buffered action profiles.}
Let $\mathcal A=\{1,\ldots,K\}$ be the registered action or skill library and let $q_i(a)$ be the
margin produced by the declared rollout from history $x_i$.  For a buffer $\gamma\geq0$, retain the
individually feasible reference set
\[
 \mathcal I_\gamma=\{i:\max_{a\in\mathcal A}q_i(a)\geq\gamma\}.
\]
For nonempty $B\subseteq\mathcal I_\gamma$, its common-action margin and violation are
\[
 c_\gamma(B)=\max_{a\in\mathcal A}\min_{j\in B}\{q_j(a)-\gamma\},
 \qquad
 v_\gamma(B)=[-c_\gamma(B)]_+.
\]
Thus $v_\gamma(B)=0$ exactly when one declared action has margin at least $\gamma$ for every member.
The audit above is the case $\gamma=0$ and $B=N_i^{\mathcal V}(\delta)$.  Profiles may come from exhaustive
simulator branching, a verified model, or a cross-fitted action-conditioned teacher, but their
source and error semantics are reported separately.  If a finite-reference teacher has a uniform
error bound $\eta$, both $c_\gamma$ and $v_\gamma$ change by at most $\eta$; a percentile error gate
does not imply this uniform statement.

\paragraph{Common-Action Geometry Repair.}
Let $R_0$ be a pretrained representation and let $R_\theta$ update modules that still access tokens
or observations before the audited bottleneck.  Write $z_i^\theta=R_\theta(x_i)$ and let
$d_\theta(i,j)$ be Euclidean distance after a calibration-frozen normalization.  For
$\delta_{\rm train}>\delta_{\rm audit}$, define
\[
 C_i^\theta(a)=
 \sum_{j\in\mathcal I_\gamma}
 [\gamma-q_j(a)]_+
 [\delta_{\rm train}-d_\theta(i,j)]_+^2,
 \qquad
 \mathcal L_{\rm CA}(\theta)
 =\frac{1}{|\mathcal I_\gamma|}
 \sum_{i\in\mathcal I_\gamma}\min_{a\in\mathcal A} C_i^\theta(a).
\]
The action minimum is replaced by a temperature-controlled soft minimum for optimization.  On a
finite reference set, a zero hard loss implies that every strict
$\delta_{\rm train}$ neighborhood restricted to $\mathcal I_\gamma$ has a common action with margin
at least $\gamma$; sampled
minibatches or mined neighborhoods do not promote this statement to the population.  Unlike a
pairwise contrastive loss, the action minimum is shared by the entire centered neighborhood and can
therefore expose higher-order conflicts whose safe-action sets intersect pairwise but not jointly.

An action-conditioned head
$\widehat q_{\theta,\psi}(x,a)=g_\psi(R_\theta(x),a)$ supplies a tail-sensitive, boundary-weighted
profile loss $\mathcal L_{\rm prof}$.  The complete objective is
\[
 \mathcal L_{\rm repair}
 =\mathcal L_{\rm native}
 +\lambda_{\rm prof}\mathcal L_{\rm prof}
 +\lambda_{\rm CA}\mathcal L_{\rm CA}
 +\lambda_{\rm retain}\mathcal L_{\rm retain}.
\]
Here $\mathcal L_{\rm native}$ retains the model's original utility and
$\mathcal L_{\rm retain}$ anchors frozen-teacher features or calibration-set pairwise geometry.
These terms are family-relative rather than a single commensurate score.  Cosmos retains
reconstruction/perceptual and rate utility through its decoder; V-JEPA retains latent prediction
and frozen-teacher features; DINO retains dense frozen-teacher feature geometry
\citep{nvidia2025cosmostokenizer,assran2025vjepa2,simeoni2025dinov3}.

\paragraph{Where repair occurs.}
For continuous Cosmos, the primary intervention places residual bottlenecks before the tokenizer
latent and evaluates reconstruction through a frozen decoder.  For V-JEPA and DINO, low-rank or
bottleneck modules enter the final internal transformer blocks before the deployed pooling and
rate constraint.  A deterministic post-code adapter $T_\theta\circ R_0$ cannot split exact
$R_0$ fibers by data processing; it is retained only as a finite-radius geometry control.  A fixed
discrete-token remapping has the same limitation, so discrete-token repair must update the encoder
or token assignment.  Appending the true or predicted profile is a side-channel upper bound, not
evidence that the original representation was repaired.

\paragraph{Separating representation repair from probe fitting.}
After post-training, we discard the joint profile head, freeze $R_\theta$, recompute the
nonparametric defect from held-out physical profiles, and fit a fresh capacity-matched readout and
downstream selector on a disjoint split.  The decisive controls are a frozen encoder with the same
head, profile-only and common-action-only internal adaptation, pairwise conflict separation, native-
loss-only adaptation, post-code adaptation, scalar-margin and FCSRL-style supervision, shuffled
profiles, conflict-count-matched random groups, and an oracle appended profile.  Unit-normalized
codes, frozen radius curves, matched-neighborhood-mass or fixed-$k$ audits, physical perturbation
groups, effective rank, distance spectra, retained viable coverage, and token entropy prevent a
trivial win by uniformly spreading codes or emptying neighborhoods.
