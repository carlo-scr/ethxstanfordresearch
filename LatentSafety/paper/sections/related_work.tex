\section{Related work and novelty boundary}

\paragraph{Pretrained visual representations.}
Cosmos provides causal image/video tokenizers trained for reconstructive and perceptual fidelity at
several spatial, temporal, continuous, and discrete compression settings
\citep{nvidia2025cosmos,nvidia2025cosmostokenizer}.  V-JEPA~2 and V-JEPA~2.1 learn dense predictive
video features; V-JEPA~2-AC adds an action-conditioned predictor for planning while retaining the
pretrained visual encoder \citep{assran2025vjepa2,murlabadia2026vjepa21}.  DINOv3 instead learns
semantic and dense spatial features from images \citep{simeoni2025dinov3}.  We use these families to
span reconstructive, predictive, and semantic objectives.  Their native metrics are not
commensurate, and checkpoint comparisons do not identify a causal effect of objective without a
matched-backbone/data ablation.

\paragraph{Task-aware physical tokenization.}
Recent work already argues that reconstruction-only driving tokens can omit planning-relevant
information and augments tokenizer learning with representation and geometry supervision
\citep{yao2026unified}.  We therefore do not claim the first task-aware, planning-aware, or
safety-augmented tokenizer.  Our narrower target is a specification-relative common-safe-action
obstruction, a traceable set-wise audit before policy learning, and internal encoder repair whose
effect survives removal of the training head in more than one physical domain.

\paragraph{Latent control certificates.}
Latent CBF and world-model methods learn certificates or safety policies from observations
\citep{anand2025safety,nakamura2025latent,nakamura2026latentcbf}.  Lutkus et al.\ give approximate-conjugacy conditions
for transferring latent Lyapunov and barrier guarantees and explicitly identify shared safe/unsafe
codes as a source of conservative under-approximation \citep{lutkus2025latent}.  Set-valued and
uncertainty-aware variants propagate zonotopes or augment the latent state with epistemic
uncertainty \citep{wu2026points,seo2025uncertainty}.  We do not claim the first latent safe set,
collision observation, latent reachability filter, latent CBF, or transfer theorem.  Our audits
precede the downstream filter and ask which feasible decisions its input representation can still
distinguish.

\paragraph{Safety-aware representations.}
Decision-sufficient and safe-RL representation learning already preserve action, reward, cost,
feasibility, or safety features \citep{huang2022action,cen2024feasibility,wang2025cvrlbm,
yang2025qsupervised,mani2025safety}.  PIGDreamer additionally uses privileged representation
alignment to improve safe partially observed world models \citep{huang2025pigdreamer}.  Our target
distinction is therefore not merely another auxiliary safety loss or privileged alignment method.
The phrase \emph{safe-action sufficiency} here denotes preservation of a nonempty common feasible
action over every represented history, rather than the structural action-sufficient variables of
\citet{huang2022action}.  The intervention directly optimizes a set-wise conflict object; profile
prediction is an ablation, not by itself the novelty.  We test it with observation-level controls,
within-family utility retention, head removal, and a held-fixed downstream controller.

\paragraph{Stochastic encoders and testing.}
Variational autoencoders represent an input by a conditional law and regularize it toward a common
prior; $\beta$-VAE changes the weight of that information bottleneck
\citep{kingma2014autoencoding,higgins2017betavae}.  Mutual singularity, total variation, binary
testing, Pinsker's inequality, and Markov-kernel data processing are classical.  We do not claim
new versions of those results.  Our use of them is to state the exact almost-sure safety semantics
of a sampled code, expose the full-support Gaussian obstruction, and connect encoder overlap to
unavoidable common-action violations.  The experiments instead audit posterior means, so sampled-
code and deterministic-mean conclusions remain separate.

\paragraph{Control abstractions and partial observation.}
Feedback refinement relations formalize controller refinement through static quantizers and
state-dependent admissible inputs \citep{reissig2017feedback}; state--action abstractions likewise
study which decisions remain representable after compression \citep{abel2020value}, while
safety-preserving approximate bisimulation quantifies conservative abstraction error
\citep{girard2012safety}.  Under partial
observation, belief-support shields already restrict actions to enforce almost-sure reach--avoid
specifications \citep{sheng2024safe}.  Common-action necessity is therefore a foundation, not our
standalone novelty.  The submission-level target is approximate safety sufficiency for learned
history representations, with calibrated audits and matched-utility intervention results.

\paragraph{History compression and approximate information states.}
Approximate information states provide learnable history compressions, latent dynamic programs,
and policy-loss bounds \citep{subramanian2022ais}; worst-case variants treat uncertain partially
observed systems and maximum-over-time costs \citep{dave2024ais}.  Approximate $Q$-profile
abstractions likewise give near-optimal policies under uniform value similarity
\citep{abel2016approximate}.  Consequently, our finite-tree Bellman calculations only define and
validate audit quantities; we do not present generic history compression, Hausdorff successor error,
or factor-two policy loss as standalone novelty.

\paragraph{Calibration and robust planning.}
Conformalized latent constraints and robust MPC address model and constraint prediction errors
\citep{nath2026pixels}; uncertainty-aware reachability targets OOD failures
\citep{seo2025uncertainty}, and partial-observation barrier-value methods combine estimator
uncertainty with finite-horizon probabilistic safety \citep{jahanshahi2026cbvf}.  These guarantees
have different semantics from support-robust information loss inside an encoder fiber.  The error
sources are conceptually distinct but interacting; their end-to-end composition remains unresolved.
